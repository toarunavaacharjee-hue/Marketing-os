Launches slip when responsibilities are unclear and assets are not reusable. This checklist focuses on the smallest set of steps that reliably produce pipeline and adoption.

## Pre-launch

- Finalize ICP, positioning, and message map
- Create one landing page and one demo narrative
- Ship sales enablement: talk track, FAQ, objection notes
- Set tracking: events, UTMs, and a weekly review dashboard

## Launch week

- Run one primary campaign and one supporting amplifier
- Hold a short daily blocker review
- Capture objections and customer questions in one place

## Post-launch

- Review performance weekly
- Update messaging with live feedback
- Turn learnings into repeatable templates and playbooks
- Define the next sprint: new angle, new audience, or new channel
