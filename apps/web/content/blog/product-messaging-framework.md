A message map is the fastest way to keep your website, outbound, ads, and sales decks consistent. If you cannot reuse your messaging across channels, it is not a framework yet.

## The six-part message map

- Audience and trigger: who is this for and what changed?
- Primary promise: the main outcome you deliver
- Three value props: the how behind the promise
- Proof: metrics, case studies, demos, comparisons
- Use cases: the workflows people buy for
- Objections: why now, why you, why not another option

## Make it operational

Do not leave the framework in a deck. Push it into:

- Landing page templates
- Email sequences
- Sales talk tracks
- Battlecards

Review it monthly using the latest wins, losses, and customer feedback.
