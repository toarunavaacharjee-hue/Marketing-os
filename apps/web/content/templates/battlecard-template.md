### Landmines *(questions to ask that expose their weaknesses)*

Ask these to shift the frame before the competitor does:

1. "How do you currently handle [pain they don't solve well]?"
2. "What happens when [edge case they struggle with]?"
3. "Have you looked at the total cost including [hidden cost]?"
