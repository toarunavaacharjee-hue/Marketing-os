### Content Calendar Principles

**Do:**
- Cluster content by theme — multiple pieces on the same topic outperform scattered coverage
- Link new content to existing high-performing pieces
- Repurpose: one long-form post → 3 LinkedIn posts → 1 email
- Plan around sales cycles and buying triggers, not just publishing cadence

**Don't:**
- Publish for volume without a keyword or audience goal
- Leave CTAs vague ("learn more") when a specific action is possible ("see the template")
- Create content without a distribution plan
- Measure content success by views alone — measure pipeline assist

---

### Review Checklist (monthly)

- [ ] Every piece has a goal and a target audience
- [ ] SEO pieces have a primary keyword with tracked ranking
- [ ] All pieces have a CTA tied to a conversion event
- [ ] Top 3 pieces from last month identified and promoted further
- [ ] Gap content identified from Search Console (high impression / low CTR)
- [ ] Content shared with sales for any enablement value
