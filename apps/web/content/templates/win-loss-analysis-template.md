## Win/Loss Analysis Template

Run win/loss interviews quarterly (or after each significant deal cycle). Aim for 5–8 interviews per quarter — a mix of wins and losses across segments.

### Interview Questions

#### Opening *(build rapport, get context)*

1. Can you walk me through your process for evaluating tools in this category?
2. Who was involved in the decision, and what did each person care about most?
3. What triggered the search in the first place?

#### Evaluation *(understand how they compared options)*

4. What were your top 3 criteria when comparing options?
5. How did you weight those criteria?
6. What did you like most about the solutions you evaluated?
7. What concerns did you have about each?

#### Decision *(understand why they chose or didn't choose you)*

8. Walk me through the moment you made the final decision. What pushed you over the line?
9. *(For losses)* What made [competitor] the right choice at that moment?
10. *(For wins)* Was there a moment you almost went with someone else? What changed?

#### Messaging *(validate or challenge your positioning)*

11. How would you describe what [your product] does to a colleague?
12. Did our messaging resonate with your situation? What felt off?
13. Was there anything we said that you found hard to believe?

#### Closing *(get the signal they wouldn't have shared in the deal)*

14. If you were advising us on how to improve, what's the one thing you'd change?
15. Is there anything I should have asked that I didn't?
