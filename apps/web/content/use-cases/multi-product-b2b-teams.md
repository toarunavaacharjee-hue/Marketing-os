When a company supports multiple products, context gets fragmented fast. Each team creates its own decks, campaigns, message maps, and reporting. Shared learning disappears.

AI Marketing Workbench is designed to help multi-product teams:

- maintain product-specific context inside one workspace
- reuse shared research across products where it makes sense
- keep launches, campaigns, and positioning easier to compare
- support clearer governance across teams and roles

## Where it helps most

- portfolio launches
- product-line positioning work
- shared research and segmentation
- centralized GTM review without flattening product nuance
