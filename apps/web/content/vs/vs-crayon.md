## AI Marketing Workbench vs Crayon

Crayon is a best-in-class competitive intelligence platform. AI Marketing Workbench is a full PMM operating system that includes competitive intelligence as one of its modules. The question isn't which is better — it's what you actually need.

---

### What Crayon does well

- **Automated monitoring:** Tracks competitor websites, pricing pages, job postings, social, and review sites continuously
- **Change detection:** Alerts when a competitor changes a key page or messaging
- **Battlecard creation:** Templated battlecard builder with CI data piped in
- **Slack integration:** Delivers intelligence to where teams already work
- **Large-scale tracking:** Tracks dozens of competitors simultaneously across hundreds of signals

If you're in a fiercely competitive market with 10+ competitors making constant moves, Crayon's automated monitoring capability is hard to beat.

---

### What Crayon doesn't do

Crayon is focused on monitoring and alerting. It doesn't help you:

- **Build positioning:** No positioning canvas, no differentiation framework
- **Define your ICP:** No segmentation module or PnF scoring
- **Create messaging frameworks:** No segment-level messaging pillars or value props
- **Plan a launch:** No GTM checklist, timeline, or launch brief workflow
- **Generate sales enablement:** No deal-stage playbooks, no coaching content
- **Run market research:** No synthesis of market signals into opportunity maps
- **Track win/loss patterns:** No objection library or win rate analysis

Crayon tells you what competitors are doing. It doesn't tell you what to do about it — and it doesn't help you build the strategic layer that turns that intelligence into action.

---

### The key workflow difference

**Crayon:** Competitor changes pricing page → alert fires → you see it → you decide what to do.

**AI Marketing Workbench:** You run a market research scan → competitive signals surface → positioning and battlecards update to reflect the new landscape.

Both are useful. Crayon wins on real-time alerting volume. AI Marketing Workbench wins on closing the loop from intelligence to action.

---

### Who should use which

**Choose Crayon if:**
- You're in a fast-moving market with many active competitors
- You need enterprise-grade CI with Salesforce/Slack integration
- Your primary need is staying ahead of competitor moves in real time
- You have a dedicated competitive intelligence analyst or team

**Choose AI Marketing Workbench if:**
- You're a PMM team (1–5 people) that needs strategy + CI + execution in one place
- You want CI connected to your positioning and battlecards automatically
- You need ICP, messaging, campaigns, and GTM planning alongside CI
- You don't need 24/7 alerting but do need quarterly research synthesis

**Use both if:**
- You have the budget and need continuous alerting AND a connected PMM workspace
